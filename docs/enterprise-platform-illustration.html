Genesis Durable · Product vision · Proposed architecture

AI collaborators.
A managed home.

Give AI agents an ongoing role in your organisation—not just another chat window. A platform to host them, preserve their work, and manage how their abilities evolve.

The value: continuity without starting over

An agent has a recognisable role and a persistent place to work. Replacing its running software should preserve its identity, conversation and files—not create a new collaborator.

The control: ability is granted deliberately

Select an environment, enable tools, and authorize specific systems. Installing a tool is not permission to access everything. Kubernetes supplies the hosting foundation; Genesis owns the agent lifecycle.

Product direction—not a released enterprise offering. Persistent agents, tool selection and gateway conversations are proven in the Docker prototype. Kubernetes and Flux integration, business-system access controls and production operations still need implementation and validation. This illustration does not claim tenant isolation, compliance certification, high availability or guaranteed recall.
Logical product overview for sales conversations. Authentication, storage technology, secret delivery and individual model-request routes are omitted for clarity. Git manages desired platform configuration—not agent conversations or private memory. Flux and Genesis must have distinct resource ownership; a future operator is not assumed.
