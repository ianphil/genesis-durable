A small experiment in a different kind of AI relationship

Meet Jupiter.

An AI companion with a home, an identity, and an ongoing conversation.

Not a fresh start every time you open a chat. We’re building a companion whose identity and conversation carry forward—and whose abilities grow deliberately.

01 / A character to get to know

A little thunder.
A lot of continuity.

Jupiter takes his name and character from the Roman king of the gods: dignified, protective, fair-minded, with a dry sense of humour.

His job is more down to earth. He is Ian’s fleet companion, learning about the computers, services and other assistants that make up this little world.

The mythology is a personality, not a claim of divine powers. He still needs to say “I don’t know.”

“Greetings, Ian. The thunder is still here, and the record of this conversation with it.”

Jupiter, after a continuity check through the fleet’s communication system.

02 / Somewhere to belong

His own room.
Not his own planet.

Jupiter lives on a Mac mini, with a private workspace for his identity and knowledge files, alongside a saved conversation.

Think of it as a room that stays put when the lights go off. Restarting his software shouldn’t mean throwing away his belongings.

His home is local, but the AI model he uses is reached through a shared gateway. This is not a claim that all the thinking happens on the Mac.

03 / Memory, without the magic

Saved is not the same
as always remembered.

There are three different things we might casually call “memory.” Keeping them separate makes the promise more honest.

01

Who he is

Identity files describe his character, purpose and boundaries. He can read them to orient himself.

02

What was said

The hosting system saves the conversation. We verified that Jupiter could recall an earlier exchange after a restart.

03

What is worth keeping

Curated notes can preserve useful knowledge. Jupiter can now write and edit notes in his workspace, alongside the saved conversation.

A long conversation won’t all fit in his immediate attention. Older context may be summarised, so a saved transcript does not guarantee perfect recall.

04 / Part of a larger world

One companion.
A fleet around him.

The fleet is a group of computers, services and AI assistants with different responsibilities. A shared communication system lets us reach an assistant wherever it lives.

Today, other assistants can contact Jupiter and receive his replies. He doesn’t yet have tools to independently find or contact them.

05 / Trust before power

Knowing the fleet
isn’t controlling it.

A title doesn’t grant permission. Calling Jupiter a fleet companion doesn’t give him the keys to your computers.

Available today

A companion with coding tools.

  • Respond through the shared communication system.
  • Read, write and edit workspace files.
  • Run commands inside his permitted container.
  • Discuss what we teach him in a saved conversation.
Possible next steps · not enabled

More ability, with specific boundaries.

  • Build a deliberate habit of curating fleet knowledge.
  • Reach out to other fleet assistants.
  • Take approved actions with explicit permissions.

His shell runs inside a container, not on the host. It can reach readable credentials, writable state and allowed network routes; it is not restricted to note-taking. He receives no Docker or gateway administration access. You set the direction.

The bigger question

What could an AI collaborator become if we built a relationship—not just another chat?

That’s the experiment. A recognisable character. A persistent home. Useful responsibilities. And room to learn together.

Jupiter is a beginning, not a finished promise.

Curious about what’s under the hood?

Genesis supplies the mind’s starting structure: identity, character and knowledge files. Genesis Durable provides the home and connects the pieces.

Pi Durable manages execution and saved conversation in SQLite. Docker separates the agent runtime from other services. Jupiter receives neither Docker administration nor gateway administration credentials.

LiteLLM is the shared model gateway, and fleet A2A routes conversations to Jupiter. His current model is Haiku 5.5.

Demigod updates the hosting service after a signed notification from a successful GitHub image build. Updating that service does not silently regenerate Jupiter’s identity or upgrade his separately pinned agent runtime.

This is a private development setup, not a claim of production hardening. The agent persona is a designed interaction—not evidence of consciousness or independent authority.
